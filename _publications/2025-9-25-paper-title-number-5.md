Using the dinuclear system model, This study examined the reaction systems employing 242Cm to 248Cm targets with 45Sc, 50Ti, 51V, 54Cr, and 55Mn projectiles, providing possible reaction systems toward elements 119–121. The predicted maximum evaporation-residue cross sections generally increase as the curium target becomes more neutron rich, while also showing a clear odd-even staggering pattern. By separating the capture, fusion, and survival stages, the study shows that the isotope dependence arises mainly from the survival process. 247Cm consequently emerges as a promising candidate target for future experiments.
